# Supply Chain Analytics Portfolio

A hands-on portfolio demonstrating how raw operational data can be transformed into business decisions through analytics, forecasting, planning, inventory optimization, and replenishment strategies.

This repository follows a real-world supply chain planning workflow used by global organizations to move from historical sales data to actionable inventory and replenishment decisions.

The objective is not simply to analyze data but to understand how data supports planning, inventory management, and operational decision-making across the supply chain.

---

# Portfolio Objective

Organizations generate massive amounts of operational data every day.

However, data alone does not create business value.

Supply chain teams must:

- Understand demand behavior
- Forecast future demand
- Create demand plans
- Optimize inventory
- Execute replenishment decisions
- Measure operational performance

This portfolio demonstrates that complete process using practical business analytics techniques and real-world supply chain concepts.

---

# Business Scenario

The projects are based on retail data from Walmart.

The portfolio addresses critical business questions such as:

### Demand Understanding

- How does demand behave?
- Is demand stable or highly variable?
- What seasonal patterns exist?
- Which events influence sales?

### Forecasting

- What will future demand look like?
- How accurate are forecasts?
- Which forecasting approaches perform best?

### Planning

- How should future demand be planned?
- How should seasonality and events be incorporated?
- How can planners improve forecast quality?

### Inventory

- How much inventory should be stocked?
- What service level should be maintained?
- What is the optimal safety stock?

### Replenishment

- When should orders be placed?
- How much should be ordered?
- How can stockouts be prevented?

### Operations

- How effectively is the supply chain performing?
- Which KPIs should be monitored?
- Where are operational improvement opportunities?

# Project Breakdown

## Project 0 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### Objective

Build a complete understanding of demand behavior before forecasting.

### Topics Covered

- Descriptive Statistics
- Demand Variability
- Weekday Analysis
- Monthly Seasonality
- Event Analysis
- SNAP Analysis
- Trend Analysis
- Correlation Analysis

### Business Outcome

Identifies key demand drivers and establishes forecasting readiness.

---

## Project 1 — Sales Forecasting

### Objective

Generate future demand forecasts using historical sales patterns.

### Topics Covered

- Naive Forecasting
- Moving Average Forecasting
- Trend Analysis
- Seasonal Forecasting
- Forecast Error Measurement
- Forecast Accuracy KPIs

### Business Outcome

Provides expected future demand to support planning decisions.

---

## Project 2 — Demand Planning

### Objective

Convert statistical forecasts into business-ready demand plans.

### Topics Covered

- Seasonal Adjustments
- Event-Based Adjustments
- Demand Drivers
- Forecast Overrides
- Planning Assumptions
- Consensus Planning Concepts

### Business Outcome

Creates a realistic demand plan aligned with business conditions.

---

## Project 3 — Inventory Optimization

### Objective

Determine optimal inventory policies based on demand behavior.

### Topics Covered

- Safety Stock
- Reorder Point
- Service Levels
- Inventory Risk Analysis
- Demand Variability Impact
- Inventory Cost Trade-Offs

### Business Outcome

Balances product availability with inventory investment.

---

## Project 4 — Replenishment Planning

### Objective

Translate inventory policies into ordering decisions.

### Topics Covered

- Replenishment Cycles
- Order Quantities
- Inventory Position Monitoring
- Order Timing
- Inventory Coverage
- Stockout Prevention

### Business Outcome

Ensures inventory arrives at the right location, at the right time, in the right quantity.
